How to Use GovAlert

📌 GovAlert

GovAlert is a web and mobile application designed to improve communication between citizens and government departments by enabling users to report public service issues such as potholes, broken streetlights, sanitation problems, and more.

The app allows users to:

- Submit reports with descriptions, photos, GPS location, and urgency tags.
- Track complaint status in real-time.
- Receive notifications about updates.
- Provide feedback after issue resolution.

It supports multiple languages (Arabic & English) and aims to increase transparency, responsiveness, and citizen engagement in municipal services.

📋 1. User Guide – For Citizens

🔹 Step 1: Access the Website
Open your browser and go to the GovAlert website URL.
You will land on the homepage, where you can:
View the project overview
Click “Sign Up” or “Log In”

🔹 Step 2: Register an Account
To submit reports, users must create an account:

Click “Sign Up”
Fill in the required fields:
Full name
Email address
Password (must meet policy requirements)
Phone number
Preferred language (Arabic or English)
Click “Register”

A confirmation email will be sent. Click the verification link to activate your account.
If the email already exists, the system will prompt you to try again or recover your password.

🔹 Step 3: Log In
Once registered and verified:

Go to the login page
Enter your email and password
Click “Login”

If login fails due to incorrect credentials, the system shows an error message and allows retry.
After a period of inactivity, the system logs you out automatically.

🔹 Step 4: Submit a Report
You can now report public service issues:

Click “Report Issue”
Fill in the form:
Title and description of the issue
Category (e.g., Road, Water, Electricity)
Optional: Upload one or more photos
Location: Automatically captured via GPS (if permission is granted), or manually entered
Urgency level (Low, Medium, High)
Click “Submit”

The system timestamps and stores your report in the database.

🔹 Step 5: Track Your Reports
After submitting a report:

Go to “My Reports”
View all submitted reports:
Report title
Date submitted
Current status (Pending, In Progress, Resolved)
Assigned department

Click on any report to view full details and comments from the government side.

🔹 Step 6: Receive Notifications
The system sends real-time updates via push notifications or in-app messages:

When the status changes (e.g., from "Pending" to "In Progress")
When a government employee adds a comment or requests clarification
During scheduled maintenance or downtime

If push notifications fail temporarily, updated status will appear when you open the app next.

🔹 Step 7: Provide Feedback
After a report is marked “Resolved”, you’ll be prompted to:

Rate the service (1 to 5 stars)
Add a short comment about your experience

This feedback helps improve the system and government responsiveness.

🔹 Step 8: Log Out
At any time, click “Logout” to securely end your session.

🛠️ 2. Admin Guide – For Government Employees

🔹 Step 1: Access the Admin Dashboard
Go to the admin login page
Enter your email and password
Select your department
Click “Login”

🔹 Step 2: View Assigned Reports
On the dashboard:

All reports assigned to your department are listed
Sort by:
Date
Urgency
Type
Status

🔹 Step 3: Manage Reports
For each report:

Click to view full details
Update the status (Pending → In Progress → Resolved)
Add comments or request clarification from the user
Assign the report to another department if needed

🔹 Step 4: Export Data
Administrators can export reports and user feedback:

Click “Export”
Choose format: CSV or PDF
Download for record-keeping or internal review

🔹 Step 5: Log Out
Click “Logout” to securely end your admin session.
